**You can build Custom Tools, use them on your own account, and share them for free. You cannot sell them, charge for them, or make money from them in any way, and you cannot use them to cheat, to harm the Game or other players, or to copy the Game.**

"Custom Tool" and "mod" mean anything made by players or third parties that uses the API or Backend, or builds on Game Content: bots, agents, dashboards, overlays, Discord bots, spreadsheets, mobile or terminal clients, route planners, calculators, data viewers, alternative front-ends, wrappers, libraries and bindings.

Custom Tools that make **no connection** to the Backend and use only information a player types in, such as a standalone spreadsheet calculator, are covered by B5 (Game Content) but not by the API Terms.

## B3. What you can do

Provided you follow the API Terms and this Part, you can:

- make guides, videos, streams, fan art, wikis and written content about the Game, including monetized video platforms' ordinary partner programs, **provided** the content is not itself a Custom Tool and you are not selling access to Game Content, Credentials or the Backend.
