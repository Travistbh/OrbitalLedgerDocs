The short version: **we do not collect personal information.** The Game identifies you by a **User ID**, a numeric or alphanumeric code. We don't ask for your name, address, phone number, date of birth, payment details or government ID. This page explains what that means in practice.

## 1. What we collect

- **User ID.** When you sign in through a third-party sign-in provider (for example Google or Discord), we receive an identifier from that provider and store it as your User ID. We use it as your account key. We don't use your provider email address, real name or profile details as our account key, and we don't ask for more than the sign-in needs.
- **Display name and avatar.** A game name such as "Pilot 1234" and an optional avatar string you or the sign-in provider may supply. Treat these as public: other players can see them.
- **Game data.** What you do in the Game: your corporation, ships, facilities, trades, messages, chat posts, news and feedback you submit. Some of this is visible to other players by design (market, chat, rankings, and any table marked Public or Global).
- **Policy acceptance.** Whether you have accepted the API Terms of Service, the EULA and Modding Policy, and this Privacy Policy, stored on your account as a 0/1 flag for each.
- **Technical data.** Standard server logs such as time of request, IP address and browser type, kept for security, abuse prevention and debugging. We don't use them to build advertising profiles.

We do not collect payment information (the Game has none), precise location, contacts, or any sensitive personal information. Third party payment providers may have different policies. Consult your third party payment provider for details. 

## 2. Why a User ID can still identify you

A User ID is not your name. But it is a **persistent identifier tied to one account**, and it can be linked to a real person if we are legally required to do so, for example to respond to a valid court order or legal process, to investigate fraud, abuse or violation of our terms, to protect the safety of people or the Game, or to establish or defend legal claims. We may use your User ID for those purposes, and we may disclose it, together with related account and log data, to the extent required or permitted by law.

## 3. How we use data

To run the Game; to keep it secure and fair; to enforce our terms (including banning cheating, resale of tools and abuse); to fix bugs and balance the Game; and to respond to legal obligations. We don't sell your data, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't use it to train AI models.

## 4. Sharing

- **Other players:** your display name, in-game activity and anything you post publicly or send to them.
- **Service providers:** hosting, networking and sign-in providers who process data for us to run the Game (for example your sign-in provider, and the hosting and tunnel services the Game runs on). They handle data under their own terms and policies.
- **Legal and safety:** as described in Section 2.
- **Business transfer:** if the Game or business is sold or transferred, account data may go with it, under this policy.

Custom Tools made by players are not ours. A tool you authorize can read and act on your account data. Only authorize tools you trust.

## 5. Retention and deletion

We keep account and game data while your account is active and as needed for the purposes above. You can ask us to delete your account and the data tied to your User ID by emailing independentsolutionsltd.2018@gmail.com from, or with proof of control of, the sign-in account. We may keep limited records where the law requires or for security and dispute purposes. Game worlds can be reset or wiped at any time (see the EULA).

## 6. Security

We use reasonable measures, including signed credentials and server-enforced access rules. No system is perfectly secure and we can't guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials and pairing codes secret.

## 7. Children

The Game is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can contact us to ask what is stored against your User ID, to correct it, or to delete it. Residents of some places (for example Colorado) have additional privacy rights. We honor those requests as the law requires. Because we don't collect personal information beyond the User ID, there is little to access or delete beyond your account and game data.

## 9. Third-party sites

The Game's page may be embedded on or linked from sites such as itch.io or Discord, which have their own privacy policies and may collect data we never see.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version and may ask you to review and accept it again in the Game. Continued play after that means you accept it.
